# Code of conduct

## The rule

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying.

## What that means here

- Many people here are not native English speakers. Do not correct someone's
  grammar unless they asked.
- Review the code, not the person.
- If you disagree, say what you would do instead and why.
- No promotion, no spam, no off topic arguments.

## Not accepted

Harassment, insults, or personal attacks. Publishing someone's private
information. Sexual language or imagery.

## Reporting

Email unclecode@kidocode.com. I read them and I will not share your name.

I can delete comments, close pull requests, and block accounts. I will explain
why when I do.
